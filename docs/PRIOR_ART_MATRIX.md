# Prior Art Matrix

| Work / Reference | Target Problem | Mathematical Model | Algorithmic Mechanism | Formal Guarantees | Protocol Layer & Target | Impact on Proposed Novelty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oliveira, Raposo, Luís (IEEE OJCOMS 2026)** | Characterization of BA window stalls in Wi-Fi 7 MLO | Empirical simulation only (no closed-form equations) | Evaluates default ns-3 STR MLO; suggests retransmission steering as future work | None | Layer 2 MAC (ns-3 model) | Identifies empirical problem; leaves analytical modeling, cross-flow isolation, and scheduling algorithms completely open. |
| **MediaTek Patent US12470496B2 / US20240244007A1** | Eliminating HOL blocking across distinct traffic flows sharing a BA window | None (patent specification) | **Receiver-side** packet parsing (inspects IP header/DSCP/descriptor) to maintain independent reordering buffers per traffic stream | None | Receiver L2/L3 interface | **Vetoes receiver-side reordering** as a novel contribution. Mandates that our solution must be strictly **sender-side** under standard receiver semantics. |
| **Patent US20260113165A1** | Multi-link Block Ack buffer management and WinStart handling | None | Receiver buffer window tracking across links | None | Receiver BA tracking | Restricts receive buffer modifications; reinforces need for transmitter-driven coordination. |
| **Patent US20250016104A1** | TID spreading across multi-link devices | None | Static/dynamic mapping of TIDs to subset of links | None | Transmitter TID mapping | Shows prior art on link spreading by TID, but does not address intra-TID multi-flow sequence interleaving. |
| **Carreño et al. (arXiv:2105.10199, 2021)** | Traffic allocation across links in 802.11be | Heuristic simulation comparison | Congestion-aware link selection (assign flow to emptiest link) | None | Transmitter U-MAC | Proves multi-link can be worse than single link; does not address sequence numbers or BA reordering delays. |
| **Al-Hraishawi et al. (arXiv:2212.12420, 2022)** | Delay distribution of MLO under finite load | Queueing-theoretic Markov chain for MAC service time | Analysis of EDCA access delay across links | Stationary delay distribution of link access | L-MAC channel access | Models link transmission delay, but omits the Block Ack reorder buffer and out-of-order sequence stalls. |
| **Sangeetha et al. (Computer Networks 2026)** | Performance of MLO with unsaturated queues | Continuous-time Markov queueing model | Queue occupancy and channel access analysis | Average queue delay | L-MAC / U-MAC queueing | Analyzes queue lengths; does not model sequence-number reordering or cross-flow HOL blocking. |
| **Choi et al. (Computer Networks 2025)** | In-device coexistence (IDC) interference in NSTR MLO | Mixed-integer linear programming for packet allocation | Alternating (ATX) and Synchronous (SATX) transmission scheduling | Optimal packet split under collision-free constraints | U-MAC / L-MAC coordination | Solves NSTR half-duplex blinding; orthogonal to STR sequence-space reordering stalls. |
| **MPTCP Schedulers (BLEST, LowRTT, Peekaboo)** | Packet reordering across heterogeneous IP interfaces | Heuristic delay-window tracking | Blocking estimation before scheduling on high-RTT subflow | Heuristic / regret bounds on transport throughput | Layer 4 (Transport) | Operates at TCP/IP layer with end-to-end RTTs and socket buffers; cannot manage 802.11 MAC sequence numbers or hardware BA bitmaps. |
| **Our Proposed Work (SASP / MLO-CFHOL)** | Cross-flow HOL blocking under shared per-TID sequence space in Wi-Fi 7 MLO | **Exact stochastic reorder model, orthogonal $(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{X})$ decomposition, BA stall boundary, and sub-Gaussian threshold theorem** | **Sender-side Sequence-Aware Stochastic Partitioning (SASP)**: Joint flow selection, link mapping, and predictive sequence assignment | **Provable lower bound, optimal sequence-order threshold, and closed-form delay reduction** | **Sender-side U-MAC (unmodified standard 802.11be receiver)** | **High novelty:** First formal model, first cross-flow decomposition theorem, first compliant sender-side sequencing algorithm. |
