| **Our Proposed Work (SASP / MLO-CFHOL)** | Cross-flow HOL blocking under shared per-TID sequence space in Wi-Fi 7 MLO | **Exact trace accounting decomposition ($\Delta = \mathcal{S} + \mathcal{X}$), Poisson stall probability, and pairwise Gaussian threshold theorem** | **Sender-side Sequence-Aware Stochastic Partitioning (SASP)**: Joint flow selection, link mapping, and predictive sequence assignment | **Provable pairwise optimal threshold $u^*$, symmetric uncertainty floor, exact closed-form stall probability** | **Sender-side U-MAC (unmodified standard 802.11be receiver)** | **Defensible Novelty:** First formal trace decomposition, first closed-form stall probability for 802.11be BA window, first compliant sender-side sequencing algorithm. |
