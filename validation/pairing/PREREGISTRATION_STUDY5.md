## 1. Analytic statements (proved in the paper; tested, not "confirmed", by the studies below)
Ideal resequencer: packets released in sequence-number (SN) order, no forced window advance. A_j = physical arrival time of packet j.
- T1 (release law): the release time of packet k is R_k = max over SN j <= k of A_j.
- T2 (airport structure): for a delivered packet k of flow i let b_k = max(A_k, max own-flow predecessors' A) and, for every other flow j, l_{j,k} = max(0, max A over flow-j packets with SN < k, minus b_k). For any coalition T of other flows kept in the trace (physical arrivals frozen, SN renumbered), the cross-blocking is X_k(T) = max_{j in T} l_{j,k}. This is the airport game (cost of a coalition = cost of its largest member).
- T3 (closed-form Shapley blame): with l sorted ascending l_(1) <= ... <= l_(m), the Shapley value of the m other flows is phi_(r) = sum_{s=1..r} (l_(s) - l_(s-1)) / (m - s + 1), l_(0) = 0. It is efficient (sum = max l = X_k), symmetric, null-player safe, rank preserving, satisfies phi_j <= l_j, lies in the core, is additive over packets, and costs O(m log m) per packet against 2^(m-1) replays.
- T4 (masking and sub-additivity): leave-one-out blame is X_k - max_{others} for the unique maximiser and 0 for every other flow; sum_j LOO_j <= X_k <= sum_j l_j.
- T5 (stability): ||phi(l') - phi(l)||_1 <= 2 ||l' - l||_1; if all arrival timestamps move by at most e, then ||l' - l||_inf <= 2e. The releaser rule (all of X to the arg-max flow) has no such bound: l = (x, x+d) versus (x+d, x) changes it by 2x for every d > 0.
- T6 (queueing is super-additive): in a FIFO M/G/1 server shared by Poisson flows the mean waiting time of a victim is a supermodular function of the set of co-located flows (Pollaczek-Khinchine), so sum_j LOO_j >= total. Together with T4: reordering is sub-additive, queueing super-additive, and R - 1 = [(R_Q - 1) E_Q - (1 - R_X) X] / E for the interventional decomposition E = E_Q + X (E_Q = Delta Net + Delta S).
- T7 (stochastic closed form): Poisson flows (rates lambda_j), independent per-packet path delays with flow-specific law F_j, infinite-server paths, shared SN space in send order. With mu_j(x) = E[(d_j - x)^+] and Lambda_j = lambda_j mu_j, the expected cross-blocking of a tagged packet of flow i with coalition T present is v(T) = integral_0^inf F_i(x) exp(-Lambda_i(x)) (1 - exp(-sum_{j in T} Lambda_j(x))) dx, and its expected Shapley blame is phi_j = integral F_i e^{-Lambda_i} (1 - e^{-Lambda_j}) [integral_0^1 prod_{k != j} (1 - t (1 - e^{-Lambda_k})) dt] dx.
- Earlier results used as bridges (Studies 2-4): accounting identity E = Delta Net + Delta S + X; boundary condition (Proposition 3); certificate r >= q.

## 2. Hypotheses, data, thresholds
All errors are max absolute differences. Seeds are fresh. Intervals are percentile bootstraps over seeds (10,000 resamples, default_rng(20261031)) unless stated.

H16 (exact oracles on simulator traces and on two other generators). Cells (each 100 seeds, 26000-26099, keyed design, 300 ms, fer 0.01): C1 S4 FIFO (load 1.0, link-2 extra delay 0.1 ms); C2 S4 FullSASP (1.0, 0.1); C3 S4 FIFO (0.75, 3.0); C4 S8 FIFO (1.0, 0.1); C5 S8 FIFO (1.0, 3.0). Generators G2 (5 streams multiplexed on one ordered connection, base delay U[10,12] ms, loss probability 0.02 with geometric retransmission delay of 50 ms per loss) and G3 (4 flows over 3 paths with Pareto(shape 1.5, scale 1 ms) delays), 100 traces each (seeds 26600-26699), 300 packets, passed through the unmodified BlockAckReceiver with an unbounded window. In every run with zero forced window advances: O1 release time equals the prefix maximum of A over SN (error <= 1e-9); O2 cross-blocking equals max_j l_{j,k} (<= 1e-9); O3 closed-form Shapley equals subset-enumeration Shapley on a random sample of 100 packets per run (default_rng(seed)) and sums to X_k (<= 1e-9); O4 for three random (victim, coalition) draws per run, the cross-blocking of the victim's packets after deleting the other flows' packets from the trace and re-running the unmodified receiver equals max_{j in T} l_{j,k} (<= 1e-9); O5 (simulator cells only) the releaser blame computed from l equals BlameReceiver's per-pair blame (<= 1e-9); O6 sum LOO <= X <= sum l on every packet (tolerance 1e-12) and the LOO formula of T4 holds; O7 core membership on 20 random subsets per sampled packet (tolerance 1e-12). H16 is confirmed if every error is within tolerance in every such run and the fraction of simulator runs with forced advances is <= 5% (otherwise reported as conditional); the number of excluded runs is reported. Descriptive: wall time per seed of closed-form blame versus replay Shapley.

H17 (reordering sub-additive, queueing super-additive in the interventional decomposition). Cells A: S4 FIFO (1.0, 0.1), seeds 26200-26299; B: S4 FIFO (0.75, 3.0), seeds 26300-26399; all 15 coalitions replayed (keyed design), delivered-packet means of Net, S, X, e2e per victim and coalition. For each cell: Delta Z_i = Z_i(N) - Z_i({i}), LOO_{j->i}^Z = Z_i(N) - Z_i(N minus j), R_Z = sum_{i,j} LOO^Z / sum_i Delta Z_i (ratio of sums over seeds and victims), for Z in {Q = Net + S, X, e2e}. H17a: the seed-cluster CI lower bound of R_Q exceeds 1 in both cells. H17b (exact): in every full-run trace of H16 (simulator cells) sum_j LOO_j <= X holds for every packet (this is O6). H17c (descriptive, no threshold): R_X, R_e2e and the check |(R_e2e - 1) E - [(R_Q - 1) E_Q - (1 - R_X) X]| <= 1e-9 (algebraic identity). Validity: V13 replay-signature violations (same arrival signature per flow across coalitions) = 0; V14 identity gap |E - (Delta Net + Delta S + X)| <= 1e-9.

H18 (stability under timestamp noise). Full-run traces of cells C1, C2, C3 with seeds 26400-26449 (150 runs). For e in {0.01, 0.1, 1.0} ms add independent U(-e, e) noise to every packet's arrival timestamp (default_rng(26400 + 1000*cell + 10*seed_index + e_index)), recompute lateness. H18a (exact): for every delivered packet ||phi' - phi||_1 <= 2 ||l' - l||_1 + 1e-12 and ||l' - l||_inf <= 2e + 1e-12 (zero violations). H18b: over packets with X > 0 in the unperturbed trace, the per-packet L1 change of the releaser blame vector exceeds that of the Shapley blame vector: the seed-cluster CI lower bound of the mean difference (releaser minus Shapley) is > 0 for every e and every cell. Reported otherwise per cell.

H19 (stochastic closed form, T7). Eight parameter sets (rates per ms; delays are mixtures of links given as (probability, base ms, exponential-jitter mean ms)): D_f = [(1,1,0.3)], D_s = [(1,3,0.5)], D_m = [(.5,1,.3),(.5,3,.5)], D_d = [(1,2,0)], D_h = [(.8,1,.2),(.2,6,2)]. P1: victim D_f, others [D_s], rates [0.2, 0.1]. P2: D_f; [D_s, D_m]; [0.2, 0.1, 0.15]. P3: D_m; [D_f, D_s, D_d]; [0.15, 0.1, 0.1, 0.2]. P4: D_m; [D_m, D_m, D_m]; [0.2, 0.2, 0.2, 0.2]. P5: D_f; [D_h, D_h]; [0.3, 0.05, 0.05]. P6: D_d; [D_f, D_s]; [0.1, 0.3, 0.1]. P7: D_h; [D_f, D_m, D_s]; [0.05, 0.25, 0.15, 0.1]. P8: D_f; [D_s, D_s, D_s, D_s]; [0.2, 0.05, 0.05, 0.05, 0.05]. For each, 100,000 Monte Carlo draws of the infinite-server resequencer (default_rng(26500 + index)) give the mean cross-blocking v(T) for every non-empty coalition T and the mean airport-Shapley blame of every other flow. H19 is confirmed if every closed-form quantity is within 4 Monte Carlo standard errors of its estimate (max |z| reported), the closed-form Shapley values sum to v(all) within 1e-6 relative, and doubling the quadrature grid changes any closed-form value by < 1e-6 relative (V16).

## 4. Claims NOT made
No claim for real 802.11be hardware, finite-window or timeout behaviour beyond the stated zero-forced-advance condition; no claim that Shapley is the unique sensible blame (Shapley's axioms are a modelling choice; they characterise it on airport games per Dubey 1982); no claim of decision utility (Study 3 X7 found none); no claim that frozen-trace blame equals interventional blame outside the boundary condition; the Poisson infinite-server model is stylised and is not claimed to describe the simulator; no claim that cross-blocking is large or small in practice.
